Start with the first nine candles. Find the tallest wick and keep your eye on that level as you advance the chart. Which candle first trades above it? Where does that candle close? Turn on the markup when you want to check.

## What to look for

In this hand-drawn example, candle six sets a high of 66. The next few candles fall away. On the way back up, candle thirteen reaches 63. It gets close enough to make the next click interesting, but the old high still stands.

Candle fourteen reaches 80 and closes at 62. Its wick crossed 66, while its close landed back below that level. That crossing and return are the sweep marked on the chart. The drop on candle fifteen is a separate observation: you could only see it one click later.
